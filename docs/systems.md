# Game systems

Numbers and lists are deliberately not repeated here: the source of truth is `Config.luau`, `ZoneConfig.luau`, `Shop/init.luau`, `Maps.luau`. This file covers only how the systems are built and what is not obvious from the code. The mark `[unverified]` means it cannot be confirmed from the code or in Studio.

## Match loop

1. `LeaderstatsServer` loads the player through `PlayerData`, creates `leaderstats` (Cash, Backpack), `ToolServer` gives out the tools.
2. The player clears snow: `ToolClient` → `BlowState` or `ToolClick` → `BlowServer.cutSnow`. The snow goes into the backpack or, with the Snow Pusher, into a pile.
3. The backpack is sold in `Snowmelter.SellZone` (`MelterServer`); a pile on a manhole pays Cash right away (`PileServer`).
4. Cash is spent on tools, their upgrades and the backpack.
5. All zones of a stage are cleared → the next stage opens. All zones are cleared → `MatchState = "finished"`, `MatchServer` sells what is left in the backpacks, opens a 20-second window to choose "Play again" / "Lobby", saves and teleports (see "Other").

## Snow

- **Snow fields.** A zone is several fields (`ZoneConfig.Zones[].Fields`). A field lies at one height; its shape is the `Areas` rectangles minus `Cuts`, so snow reaches the walls, and the porch, the steps and the shed floor are separate fields. The porch with its steps and the shed are also separate zones; the back porch stays in `Backyard`: it is behind the house and opens with the backyard. Neighbouring fields share the line under a fence or gate; their common noise seed matches on that line.
- **`SnowField`** — the server heightmap of one field, step `SnowGridRes`. Point index `ix * nz + iz`. Fresh snow = (noise in world coordinates + drift at the shape edge × `Drift`) × the `Depth` thickness of the rectangle. Thickness blends smoothly between rectangles.
- **Obstacles.** There is no snow under parts with the `SnowBlock` attribute (`blockParts`, rotation taken into account, a Cylinder is a circle) and in `Cuts`. An object without the attribute stands right in the snow. Around manhole covers `meltDiscs` makes a round thawed patch with a soft edge.
- **Portions.** A zone is divided into portions: all its snow / `SnowPortionVolume`; the value is in the zone's IntValue `Total`, so it changes a little from match to match. Snow volume / `Total` = 1 portion = +1 to Backpack and Score. A remainder smaller than a portion accumulates in `carry[player]`.
- **End of a zone.** When ≤1% of the snow of all the zone's fields is left, but no less than half a portion (for small zones), the zone is cleared out and gets `cleared`. `State = "finished"` comes only when there are no piles inside its shape (`tryFinish`, every 0.5 s). A pile pushed into the house wall does not hold the zone.
- **States and stages.** `locked` → `active` → `finished`. Zones with the same `Stage` open together: Yard, Porch and Shed at once, the rest one by one; the next stage opens when all zones of the previous one are cleared. Opening hides the gate `ZoneConfig.Gate`, its snow `<Gate>_Snow` and the sign on the gate.
- **Network (`SnowSync`).** The client asks for `"full"` (no more than once per 2 s); the server answers `("full", fieldIndex, buffer)` for each field (index in `ZoneConfig.Fields`): u8 per point, step `SnowHeightQuant`. Changes go out as `FireAllClients("delta", fieldIndex, buffer)` (`flushDeltas`): entries of u16 index + u8 height. Hold tools: `flushDeltas` runs at the end of every melt tick (`MeltTickRate`, 15 Hz), so a cut reaches the clients in the same tick; a click (`ToolClick`) flushes right after its cut. A zone clear-out (`field:clear()`) happens inside `cutSnow`, so it goes out with that tick or click. The separate loop every `SnowSyncInterval` (0.1 s) only catches changes made outside a cut: regeneration (not called while `MATCH_MODE = true`) and any other `clear()`. `[unverified]` The traffic of 2–3 KB/s per blowing player is only an estimate.
- **`SnowMeshClient`** draws all fields of a stage as one EditableMesh in `workspace.LocalSnow.Snow_<first zone of the stage>` without collisions: players don't walk on the snow, their feet sink in it. Vertex colour depends on thickness, snow thinner than `HIDE_BELOW` hides under the ground, there is a "skirt" along the mesh edges, footprints are only a local dent. Vertices are drawn from the mesh origin, so `Position = (Center.X, GroundTopY + 0.02, Center.Z)`.
- **Easing of the ground snow (`SnowMeshClient`, e2cbcc4, 2026-11-09).** Client only. Each field keeps `srv` (heights from the server), `shown` (heights on screen) and `moving` (the active set: vertices still catching up). A changed server height puts the vertex into `moving`; every Heartbeat each moving vertex goes `shown += (target − shown) × (1 − exp(−dt / EASE_TAU))`, `EASE_TAU = 0.1` s (frame-rate independent), and when it is closer than `EASE_EPS = 0.002` to the target it snaps to it and leaves the set. With nothing moving, no vertex is written. The target is `srv`, but snow below `HIDE_BELOW` (0.08) gets the target `HIDDEN_Y / depthScale` (`HIDDEN_Y = SKIRT_BOTTOM = −0.25`), so the last bit of snow slides under the ground instead of snapping there; a negative `shown` is drawn as `shown × depthScale`. Footprints (`foot`) apply instantly, a new or rebuilt mesh starts at the current heights without easing, and the block fallback draws `srv` as it is. Vertex writes go in one batch per field per frame: `EditableMesh:BatchSetValues` once for positions and once for colours. Measured 2026-11-09 in the Yard (simulated 3 s digs): the largest per-frame vertex jump went from 0.43–0.49 to 0.08–0.12 studs, the snow update costs 0.05–0.08 ms per frame on average (up to 0.24 ms); clearing the whole Yard at once moved 5,762 vertices for about 0.7 s at 1.3 ms per frame on average, 4.0 ms at most. `[unverified]` Colours on screen, walking footprints, several players digging, phone cost.
- **Visual depth.** `Config.SnowVisualDepth` (1.5, read by `SnowMeshClient` and `ShovelChunksClient`) multiplies the snow height only in rendering (the server, portions and economy do not change). In Studio variants are compared live with the workspace attribute `DebugSnowDepth` (a number); the mesh is then rebuilt. Without a rebuild, stretching all heights by the same factor is not visible: a MeshPart fits the vertices into the bounds computed at creation (tested in Play 2026-11-03). Drifts at the edges grow with the snow and bury the fence and the car (Oleg's decision 2026-11-04), except in the field's `KeepDrift` rectangles in `ZoneConfig` (the pass showcase and the porch): there, above `DRIFT_CAP` the extra height fades out smoothly, with a transition of `KEEP_BLEND` studs around the rectangle.
- **Mesh memory budget.** It is shared by the whole game and depends on the device. In Studio on 2026-11-01 the ninth mesh was not created even when the locked zones were in a sparse grid, so the number of meshes itself also uses it up. Hence one mesh per stage (4 meshes for 6 zones), and only the open one is drawn in detail: a locked one uses a grid with step `LOCKED_STRIDE`, and the mesh of a fully cleared one is deleted. The switch follows the `State` of its zones. `[unverified]` Whether this is enough on weak phones.
- **The zone label in the HUD** shows the zone under the player's feet (`ZoneConfig.zoneAt` by X and Z, polled 5 times per second); outside the zone shapes, for example on the sidewalk by the fence, the previous one stays. The journal task "clear the yard" counts only the `Yard` zone.
- **Fallback snow.** If an EditableMesh or MeshPart cannot be created, the stage is drawn as 4×4-stud blocks (`Snow_<Field>_Blocks`, no footprints). In Studio it is turned on with the workspace attribute `DebugSnowBlocks = true` before the run.
- `[unverified]` The Mesh / Image APIs setting in the Creator Dashboard (Configure → Settings) is required; Oleg turned it on on 2026-10-14.
- **`SnowParticles`** works without Parts: one Attachment in `workspace.Terrain`, `Emit()` per point. The server sends `BlowParticles` only to players closer than `ParticleRenderDistance`. It skips the shovel: that one is drawn by `ShovelChunksClient`.
- **Shovel clumps (`ShovelChunksClient`, 2026-11-04).** Client-only visuals, driven by the same `BlowParticles`: the server, the cut and the economy were not changed.
  - **Pool.** On start the client clones MeshParts from `workspace.ShovelChunkTemplates` (5 shapes, see `docs/studio-world.md`) into `workspace.LocalShovelChunks`: 24 clumps, 12 in lite mode (the maximum Oleg allowed). No new parts during play: if there is no free clump, no new one flies. Free ones wait at `PARK` (Y = −200). No templates → fallback balls and a `warn`.
  - **Don't use EditableMesh for the clumps.** Its memory budget is shared with the snow: with 5 clump meshes the Backyard snow fell back to blocks (Play 2026-11-04, `reaching memory budget limits`).
  - **Throw** once per swing (`SWING_INTERVAL`, the bob rhythm from `ToolClient`) from the accumulated cut points: 2 clumps, 4 and bigger in deep snow and drifts (`SnowBaseDepth × 1.25`). Other players' shovels throw half as many and only closer than `OTHER_DISTANCE` (40) to the camera. Size keeps the shape's proportions.
  - **Motion without physics:** an arc with `GRAVITY`, rotation; on impact a squash (`SQUASH_*`, `SETTLE_Y` remains), the rotation fades smoothly, then the clump shrinks (`SINK_SHRINK`) and settles into the snow. One `BulkMoveTo` per frame moves all clumps. Dust and crumbs (`Emit()`) only at the blade and at the impact point.
  - **Landing height** is approximate: the field ground from the `ZoneConfig.Fields` rectangle + the highest cut point × `SnowVisualDepth`. `[unverified]` At the porch, the steps and high drifts a clump may hang in the air or sink.
  - **Camera kick** for the local player: a downward nod of `SHAKE_DEGREES` (0.25°) over `SHAKE_TIME` after the CameraModule. Only the difference from the previous frame is added: in first person the CameraModule takes the direction from the current `Camera.CFrame`, otherwise the view creeps downward.
  - **Sound** (set chosen by Oleg by ear on 2026-11-05, only Pro Sound Effects from the Creator Store; ids in `LAYERS`): on a swing, the blade going in (`blade`, Wood Paddle Into Icy Snow) and a scrape (`scrape`, Snow Scrape Crispy); on a heavy swing, after 0.03–0.08 s, a crunch (`crunch`, Snow Impact Big Hits Stomping); on a clump landing, a thud (`land`, Snowball Hits Lands Semi-Deep, no more often than `LAND_GAP` per player). 8 voices (`VOICES`) — Sounds in their own Attachments in `workspace.Terrain`, group `SoundService.ShovelSfx`, everything is created on start, recordings are preloaded with `PreloadAsync`. A random recording with no immediate repeat, pitch 0.92–1.08, volume ±15%, long recordings are cut at `maxLen` with a `FADE_TIME` fade. Other players' shovels ×0.6 and in 3D (falloff 5–40 studs). No free voice → a less important one is taken (own above others', landings lowest). With `DebugShovelFxOld` there is no sound (the old shovel had none).
  - **Lite mode:** a phone without a keyboard, or a manually chosen graphics quality of 1–3. Clump shadows are off in it.
  - **Tested in Studio 2026-11-05**, Test with a server and 3 simulated clients on one PC: each client had a pool of 24, snow as meshes without blocks. Two dug, the third nearby saw their clumps (up to 10 at once), the digger had up to 12 own and others'. No errors in the server and client Output.
  - `[unverified]` Live players on different machines (real network and latency), 4 players, a real weak phone, landing on the porch, the steps and high drifts.
  - **Settings in Studio** (no effect outside Studio): workspace attributes `DebugShovelFxOld = true` — old puffs instead of clumps, live; `DebugShovelShake` — kick strength in degrees (0 — off), live; `DebugShovelLite = true` — lite mode, before Play.
  - **Dig cycle (chosen by Oleg 2026-11-08):** stab → lift → throw to the right → rest, period `SWING_INTERVAL × 1.5` ≈ 0.52 s. Poses in `ToolClient` (`SWING_KEYS`, Handle system), connected through `ReplicatedStorage.SnowGame.ShovelSwing`: on `Impact` the blade-in sound and the camera kick, on `Throw` the accumulated cut points fly and a soft scrape plays (only if the cut is fresher than 0.6 s). The animation is visual only: the server cuts snow continuously (`MeltTickRate`, 15 times per second) and sends each tick's cut in the same tick (see "Network"); the client eases toward it. Other players' shovels throw on a timer; other players do not see their animation.

## Tools

- `Config.Tools` — the order equals the hotbar slots. The fields are described in the comment above the table. `Config.getToolStats` computes the stats with upgrades, one function for server and client.
- Hand (`Bare = true`, `Config.BareTool`) is not an item: it is not put in the Backpack and works when there is no Tool in the character. Clicks without a tool are caught by `ToolClient` through `UserInputService`, and `BlowServer` treats empty hands as Hand. The upgrades and the id `Hand` are unchanged.
- `ToolServer` keeps what was bought and the levels, gives out tools after loading and on `CharacterAdded`, maintains the player attributes `OwnedTools` and `ToolUpgrades` (JSON). Everyone has the `Price = 0` tools and they are not saved.
- Buying: a tool — a ProximityPrompt on the shed shelf (`ToolShopServer` → `ToolServer.buyTool`), an upgrade — remote `BuyToolUpgrade`, the backpack — remote `BuyUpgrade` or the stall's ProximityPrompt (`UpgradeShopServer`). Cooldown 0.3 s.
- One `ToolClient` for all tools. Hold: `BlowState(true, look)` every `BlowAimInterval`, which is also the keepalive. Released, unequipped the tool, lost focus or opened a menu → `BlowState(false)`. Click: `ToolClick(look)` no more often than `Cooldown`. The bob is local only, through `RightGrip.C1`.
- On touch screens there is a "Clear" button (ru «Убирать», icon only; `PlayerGui.ToolTouchGui.UseTool`) with the icon of the tool in hand, or the Hand icon with empty hands; the release is caught by the InputObject, the finger may slide off. In Studio it is shown with the workspace attribute `DebugTouchButton = true`.
- `BlowServer`: limit `BlowStateMaxRate`, stop on `BlowKeepAlive`, without a tool and on death. Cone apex = root + `NozzleOffset`, points closer than `MinDistance` are not taken, the range is limited by the point where the look ray meets the snow. The zone is determined by the snow point, not by the player's feet.
- `Shape = "strip"` (Snowblower): instead of a cone, a strip of `StripWidth` × `Range`; the `width` upgrade widens the strip.
- **Cut profile and soft edges (`BlowServer.cutSnow`, 158fda7, 2026-11-09).** Per point, `rate = rateMax × fade × (EdgeMeltRatio + (1 − EdgeMeltRatio) × t) × (1 − 0.5 d²)`: `t` is 1 on the cone (strip) axis and 0 at its side, `d` = distance from the player / `Range` (capped at 1). `fade` replaces the old hard edges: at each boundary the rate falls off with a smoothstep over a band `CUT_EDGE_SOFT = 1.5` studs wide, centred on the old edge (`CUT_EDGE_HALF` = 0.75 inside, 0.75 outside), via `edgeFade(d)` (d = distance past the boundary, negative inside; 1 deep inside, 0 deep outside). Boundaries: `MinDistance` for both shapes; for the cone, the reach (`Range`, shortened to where the look ray meets the snow) and the cone side (distance from the point to the cone's edge line); for the strip, the nozzle plane (nothing behind the nozzle), the reach along the look and `StripWidth / 2` sideways. The scan radius grows by `CUT_EDGE_HALF` so the outer half of the band is reached. What the band loses inside it gains outside, so the amount per tick barely changes. Economy, portions per tick (per click for the Hand), base stats, old → new, measured 2026-11-09 with a math model of `cutSnow` in Studio (it matched the real first-tick values in Play):

  | Tool | Average over 400 random positions and directions | One grid-aligned test spot in Play |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hand (per click) | 0.524 → 0.511 (−2.4%) | 0.500 → 0.514 (+2.8%) |
  | Shovel | 0.146 → 0.143 (−2.3%) | 0.134 → 0.145 (+8.3%) |
  | Snow Pusher | 0.257 → 0.252 (−1.7%) | 0.246 → 0.255 (+3.7%) |
  | Snowblower | 0.480 → 0.472 (−1.6%) | 0.512 → 0.477 (−6.8%) |

  With maxed upgrades all tools are between −1% and −4%. A single spot swings by up to about ±8% depending on how the player stands against the 1-stud grid; the old hard-edged cut swung the same way.
- `[unverified]` Speed without upgrades while moving ≈ Hand 1, Shovel 1.75, Snow Pusher 3, Snowblower 4.5 snow/s.
- **Sound of the Hand, Snow Pusher and Snowblower (`ToolSfxClient`, set 1A–7A chosen by Oleg 2026-11-08, Pro Sound Effects from the Creator Store, ids at the top of the script).** Client only; the server was not changed. The shovel is voiced by `ShovelChunksClient`.
  - **Where events come from:** the local hold — the module `ReplicatedStorage.SnowGame.ToolUse` (`ToolClient` sets it on `BlowState(true)`, confirms it with each keepalive and clears it on release; without confirmation for longer than 0.3 s there is no hold). Cut snow — the `BlowParticles` echo (it also reaches the player themself). The tool — a Tool with `SnowToolId` in the character; none means Hand.
  - **Hand:** on a cut echo a short crunch (shovel scrape recordings, pitch 1.1–1.25), no more often than `SCOOP_GAP`; an empty click is silent.
  - **Snow Pusher** (a hand scraper, no motor): the scraper loop (Wood Paddle Canoe Long) while holding and cutting snow or while the own pile moves; a thud (clump landing recordings) when the own pile crosses a whole portion of `Portions`; steam (Medium Pressure Air Release) at the nearest manhole cover when the own `ManholeCash` grows. Thud and steam only for the local player: the client does not know whose pile it is.
  - **Snowblower:** the motor loop (Zamboni Ice Surfacer, Loop) for the whole hold, spin-up 0.25 s and spin-down 0.4 s — by pitch and volume, without recordings; under it the "chewing snow" loop (Brush Spinning) while cutting and the backpack is not full. Motor pitch rises with the `Power` upgrade and the backpack fill; the "chewing" volume — with depth and the `Width` upgrade. The `SuctionLoop` sound was removed from the model (2026-11-08, in Studio): `BlowServer` played it; without it the server simply stays silent.
  - **Voices:** group `SoundService.ToolSfx`. Each player has an Attachment in `workspace.Terrain` with two loops (created on first use, removed on `PlayerRemoving`), short sounds — 6 voices (4 in lite mode), own more important than others'. Others ×0.6, 3D 5–40 studs, beyond 40 (20 in lite mode) the loops are silent; in lite mode others have no "chewing snow" and no Hand crunch.
  - **Stop:** any loop without feeding (the local hold from `ToolUse` or a fresh echo for others) fades out by itself, also on death and when the tool is unequipped.
  - **Tested in Play 2026-11-08** (one client, Tool:Activate from MCP): motor spin-up and spin-down, "chewing" only while cutting, fade after release, unequip and death, the scraper loop and thuds while pushing a pile, the Hand crunch on the echo, steam at the manhole on `ManholeCash` growth (attribute raised by hand). No errors in Output.
  - `[unverified]` By ear (volume balance, loop seams), the Roblox menu (Escape from MCP did not open the menu; the path is the same `BlowState(false)`), real melting of a pile on a manhole, other players, phone.

## Piles and manholes (`PileServer`)

- `Pile = true` (Snow Pusher): snow goes not into the backpack but into a pile in front of the player. A pile is a ball Part `SnowYard.Piles.SnowPile`, the `Portions` attribute is fractional, created at runtime.
- A pile can only be pushed, not pulled. If it runs into another one, they merge.
- A pile on a manhole melts: whoever pushed it immediately gets Cash = portions × `ManholePayRatio` × the shop multiplier; fractional remainders accumulate. `SnowCollected`, `SnowSold`, `ManholeCash`, `MatchCashEarned` grow.
- `PileTake` (Hand, Shovel): take from a pile into the backpack, in portions per second.
- Snow in a pile has already been removed from the heightmap, so it already counts toward the zone's Score.

## Pile visuals (`PileClient`, 2026-11-08/09)

- Client only; `PileServer` was not changed and stays the source of truth (position, size, merges, melting, `PileTake`).
- **Hides the server ball.** Each `SnowYard.Piles` child gets `LocalTransparencyModifier = 1` and a local clone of the template in `workspace.PileVisuals` (`SnowPileVisual`). No template (or no SurfaceAppearance in it) → one `warn` and the piles stay server balls; there is no other fallback.
- **Smoothing.** Every Heartbeat the visual follows the server with frame-rate independent exponential smoothing (`1 − exp(−k·dt)`): position `POS_K = 12`, radius `SIZE_K = 8`. A new pile grows from radius 0, and a pile that shrinks (Hand/Shovel `PileTake`) eases down to the server radius.
- **Prediction of the own pile.** While the local player holds the Snow Pusher (`ToolUse.held() == "Blower"`), the pile in front of them is found with the same rule as `PileServer.push` (look direction if it is within 90° of the body, otherwise the body; front = root + look × (r + `PilePushGap`), within r + `PileGrabDistance` + `DRIFT_MAX`) and is placed at that front point from the local character, push only, never pull. Without it the pile lagged a ping behind the scraper and caught up in jerks. Further than `DRIFT_MAX = 3` studs from the server the prediction is dropped. The switch between prediction and server blends with `BLEND_K = 6`, and the position speed goes from `POS_K` up to `PREDICT_POS_K = 40`.
- **Disappearance** (the server pile is removed): merge → the visual slides into the nearest remaining pile within (r₁ + r₂) × 0.7 + `MERGE_SLACK` (1.5); manhole → it slides to the nearest lid and sinks by `MANHOLE_SINK` (0.6) × radius; otherwise it just melts. Shrinking uses `DIE_K = 14` (about 0.25 s), at most `DIE_MAX_TIME` = 0.6 s.
- **Shape.** The mound is stretched along the push: size = (`MESH_HALF_WIDTH` 1.05 × 2r, height, `MESH_HALF_LENGTH` 1.25 × 2r), height = max(r × `MESH_HEIGHT` 1.2, min(`MESH_MIN_HEIGHT` 1.7, 2r)) so a small pile does not sink in the snow and a new one does not appear tall at once; the bottom is `MESH_BURY` (0.15) in the ground. It turns toward the direction of motion (`YAW_K = 6`, only faster than `YAW_MIN_SPEED` 0.6 studs/s), modulo π, so it does not flip around when pulled back.
- **Puffs.** One ParticleEmitter per pile (`Enabled = false`, `smoke_main.dds`, colour `PUFF_COLOR` 214, 226, 246). While the visual moves faster than `PUFF_MIN_SPEED` (1.2 studs/s), puffs are emitted with `Emit(1)` at random points on the edge of the base (the attachment is moved before each one, aimed up and outward): `PUFF_PER_STUD` 2.2 per stud of travel, at most `PUFF_MAX_RATE` 24 per second per pile, half as many in lite mode (`ToolUse.isLite()`). `[unverified]` Oleg has not yet judged the puffs by eye.
- **Template `src/house/replicated/SnowGame/PileMound.rbxmx`** (hand-written XML, synced by Rojo to `ReplicatedStorage.SnowGame.PileMound`): MeshPart, mesh `rbxassetid://134881248189180`, Anchored, CanCollide/CanQuery/CanTouch false, CastShadow true, Material Plastic, white; child SurfaceAppearance `SnowPileAppearance` with ColorMap `rbxassetid://72091145694406`, NormalMap `rbxassetid://120730468173755`, RoughnessMap `rbxassetid://129146635142822`, tint `Color` = (238, 240, 248). The maps are on a SurfaceAppearance because the built-in Snow material on a mesh is projected along the part's axes and shows seams; a SurfaceAppearance's map properties cannot be set from a script (Plugin security), hence the template.
- **Source `blender/snow_pile.py`.** A flattened dome on a 2×2 ellipse, height 1, flat bottom, a softly uneven outline (48 segments × 14 rings, about 1,300 triangles, smooth normals), one top-down UV unwrap repeated `UV_REPEAT = 3.5` times across the pile. Seamless 512×512 maps from periodic noise: colour (near white, grey; the tint comes from the SurfaceAppearance), OpenGL tangent-space normal (`NORMAL_K = 1.8`, grain band `GRAIN_CYCLES` 9–26 periods per tile), 8-bit roughness around 0.9 with rare smoother sparkles (0.55). Exports in `blender/exports/snow_pile/`: `snow_pile.fbx`, `snow_pile_color.png`, `snow_pile_normal.png`, `snow_pile_roughness.png`. Run: `blender --background --python blender/snow_pile.py -- <folder>`, or in an open Blender set `OUT` in the globals and run the file (optional globals: `MAPS_ONLY` writes only the maps, `NORMAL_K_OVERRIDE` replaces `NORMAL_K`). The uploaded asset ids above must be replaced in the template after a re-upload. `[unverified]` A faint UV stretch low on the steep sides.

## Finds and collection (`FindServer`, `FindClient`)

- Numbers and the list — `Finds.luau`. At the start of a match the server picks a random snow point in the first field of the zone for each find. The server decides everything: `BlowServer.cutSnow` reports every cut point (`onCut`), and when a zone is cleared out — `claimZone`.
- **Icicle:** sticks out of the snow; when the point under it is cut → portions go straight into the backpack, even over capacity. The Snow Pusher also picks it up into the backpack.
- **Golden snowflake:** a chance per match. It sparkles when snow nearby starts being cut; when its point is cut → Cash with the shop multiplier.
- **Lost item:** when its point is cut → the item appears with a badge and a prompt. The first player picks it up; a new one opens a slot in the collection, a repeat gives a little Cash.
- **Snowman gnome:** one random place from `GnomeSpots`, stands in the open, even behind locked gates. Each player finds it on their own, once per match.
- **Collection:** player attribute `Collection` ("Id,Id"), in the save the field `Collection`. For the whole set, one `SetCash` once. The journal tab shows all slots.
- **Testing in Studio:** the workspace attribute `DebugFinds = true` before Play puts the golden snowflake and the items in the first zone.

## Reward lights (`RewardLightsClient`)

- A zone's lights are off until the zone's `State` becomes `finished`, then they light up in a wave over a second, with sparks and a sound. The server code was not changed: the source is the same `State`, so a player who joins later sees the lights of cleared zones right away.
- A new light is a part with the `RewardLight` tag and the attributes from `docs/studio-world.md`; no code change needed. While the zone is not cleared, Neon is off; the general evening light is not touched.
- Testing in Play without clearing: on the server `SnowYard.<Zone>.State.Value = "finished"`. `BlowServer` itself will not notice this, so the method does not work for testing match progress.

## Passes in the world (`PassShowcaseClient`)

- The items and the sign stand on the map (`docs/studio-world.md`, folder `PassShowcase`); the script finds them by the `ShopPass` tag: Workspace streams in parts. A new item is a part with this tag, `ShopPassKey` and `PassPrompt`; no code change needed.
- The prompt calls `PromptGamePassPurchase`, the purchase is handled by `ShopServer`. Pass bought (`Pass_<Key>`) → the prompt is off, the caption reads "Purchased ✓", the `×2 Cash` sign is green. The sign looks only at the pass, not at the boost or the blizzard.
- On the owner's account all passes count as bought. The state without a pass is tested in Play: on the server `Pass_X2Cash = false`.

## Newcomer tasks (`OnboardingServer`)

- Steps and rewards — `Config.Onboarding`: collect snow → sell → buy the Shovel → clear the porch → upgrade the backpack → clear the shed → clear the yard. Pushing a pile onto a manhole is not in the chain: the Snow Pusher costs 30 Cash, and the step could stall the chain for a long time.
- The server counts, the client only displays: player attributes `OnboardingStep` (steps completed), `OnboardingProgress`, `OnboardingGoal`. The journal shows the same list.
- `OnboardingStep` is stored in the save, so completed steps and rewards are not repeated. Steps are identified by number: new ones only at the end of the list. The counter inside a step lives for one match.
- An already fulfilled condition (the Shovel bought, the zone cleared by the group) counts by itself, no more often than once per `STEP_PAUSE`. Players with old saves also go through the chain from the start.
- Chain completed → the card is hidden. Daily quests can be shown through `current` in `refreshHud`.

## Save and lock (`PlayerData`)

- Data: `SchemaVersion`, `Cash`, `UpgradeLevel`, `OwnedTools`, `ToolUpgrades`, `Purchases` (last 50 receipts), `BoostUntil`, `OnboardingStep`, `Collection` (find ids), `Crystals`, `Daily = {First, Claimed, Total}`, `Stats = {[Id] = level}`, `Session = {Job, Time}`.
- Who writes which field (one save key, `Shared.SaveStore`). The match save writes only the fields it knows onto the stored table, so fields of the lobby survive it (tested 2026-11-09: an autosave kept `Crystals` and `Daily`).

| Field | Written by | When |
|---|---|---|
| `Cash`, `UpgradeLevel`, `OwnedTools`, `ToolUpgrades`, `OnboardingStep`, `Collection` | House `PlayerData` | autosave, leaving, teleport |
| `Session` | House `PlayerData` | load and autosave; cleared on release |
| `Purchases`, `BoostUntil` (and `Cash` from packs) | House `PlayerData.grantReceipt` or Lobby `ShopServer` | the lobby only without a fresh `Session` |
| `Crystals` | Lobby `DailyServer` (rewards) and `StatServer` (spending) | only without a fresh `Session`; House never writes them |
| `Daily` | Lobby `DailyServer` only | only without a fresh `Session`; House never writes it |
| `Stats` | Lobby `StatServer` only | only without a fresh `Session`; House only reads them into `Stat_<Id>` |
| `SchemaVersion` | everyone, through `SaveSchema.upgrade` | every write |

- Schema version (`Shared.SaveSchema`, currently 3; no field means 0; step 2 added `Crystals` and `Daily`, step 3 an empty `Stats` table, both with their own frozen code; levels are validated when read and when bought, not in the migration): loading in the match, saving, and a purchase in the lobby bring the save to the current version inside `UpdateAsync`. A save newer than this game or a failed step → the write is cancelled (the function returns `nil`); warn and kick come after `UpdateAsync`, because the function may run several times. The match kicks with `Kick.SaveTooNew`, the lobby answers the receipt with `NotProcessedYet`. `LobbyCashServer` only reads and does not check the version.
- Only the server that loaded the player may write. Someone else's fresh lock (younger than 120 s) → wait; the 6th attempt, roughly after 15 s, takes it by force. If loading fails — Kick. In Studio without API access the game runs without saving.
- Autosave every 60 s. `BindToClose` also waits for saves started from `PlayerRemoving` (`waitForSaves(25)`).
- `MatchServer` saves the player with the lock released once, right before their teleport: on "Lobby" (in the match or in the choice window) immediately, the others at the end of the 20-second choice window. This way the save also gets what was bought or found during the window, the next server loads the player on the first attempt, and `PlayerRemoving` does not save a second time (`isLoaded` = false). After the lock is released, changes are no longer saved (important if the teleport fails). The Blizzard (`MatchOnly`) is not granted after the match ends: the receipt waits for the next match.

## Lobby

- `LobbyServer` finds the pads in `Workspace.Blockout.QueuePads.QueueN.Pad` by the `QueueIndex` attribute and determines the player on a pad geometrically. Phases: `empty` → `setup` (`SetupTime`) → `starting` (`StartTime`) → `teleporting`. Teleport: `ReserveServer` + `TeleportAsync` with `TeleportData {map}`.
- `TeleportData` goes through the client, so `MatchServer` accepts only known map ids and writes the choice to `workspace.MatchMap`.
- `QueueAction` (client → server): `setMaxPlayers`, `setMap`, `setFriendsOnly`, `confirm`, `leave`, `quickPlay`. `QueueState` (server → client): the pad state, broadcast to all lobby players. That is why `QueueAction` is limited to 5 actions per second per player, and a host setting is broadcast only if the value changed.
- "Friends only": `IsFriendsWith` in the background with a cache; strangers are pushed out.
- `quickPlay` (the PLAY button, 1 s cooldown): puts the player on the pad of a group that is filling up — `starting` first, then `setup`, among equals the one with more players, then the one with the earlier start. If there is none, takes an empty one and starts the countdown at once. Not on a pad within 2 s → toast `Lobby.Hud.NoFreePads`.
- Signs: `Sign.BoardGui.Board.Strip.Top` and `.Main`, above the pad `Pad.PadGui.Count` and `.Status`. The texts are set by `LobbyServer`; **do not rename these objects or the `QueuePads` folder.**
- `LobbyHudClient`: Cash and Crystals, PLAY, shop, invite, daily rewards (red dot while `DailyReady`), permanent upgrades, music (`SoundService.LobbyMusic`, muted locally; with an empty `SoundId` there is no button). The placeholder buttons (Clans, Classes, Stats, Feedback, Updates) were removed on 2026-11-02: add a new button together with its window.
- `LobbyMenuClient`: the "Game settings" and "Map choice" panels for the host; jump = leave the pad. The map card takes `Image` and `Icon` from `Maps` if they are set.
- The map name is the key `Map.<Id>`.
- `LobbyTruckClient`: on `QueueState` the truck's beacons blink (`setup` slower, `starting` and `teleporting` faster), during the countdown the meshes shake and there is smoke. Display only.
- `LobbyRecordsServer`: on entering the lobby and when the `Cash` attribute changes, writes the player's Cash to the OrderedDataStore `TopCash_v1` (in Studio `TopCash_Studio`, does not write with TestSave), and once per 60 s reads the top 8 into the `TopPlayers` attribute on `ReplicatedStorage.Lobby`. `PlayerData` is not changed. The table shows the current Cash, not the all-time earnings: there is no other counter in the save. A player gets into it only by entering the lobby.
- Ragdoll on the slide: `SlideRagdollServer` starts it when the character's root is inside a part with the `SlideStart` tag, and ends it when the character has rolled to a stop (slower than 5 studs/s for half a second, but not earlier than 1 s), and in any case after 6 s. It turns off the animation joints (`AnimationConstraint` of the new rig, `Motor6D` of the old one) and turns on limb collision. The character attribute `Ragdoll` is read by `SlideRagdollClient`: the Humanoid state and the tumble are controlled by the character's owner.
- Snowballs: `SnowballClient` (LMB on the world, a button on touch screens) sends only the direction to `ThrowSnowball`. `SnowballServer` checks its type and rate, computes the flight and the hit with a ray itself, `SnowballFx` shows them to everyone. No damage and no knockback, so nobody can knock a player off a pad. Numbers in `Lobby/Snowball.luau`.

## Daily reward (`DailyServer`, `Lobby.DailyGui`, 2026-11-09)

- **Crystals** are the persistent currency (Cash will later become per-match). For now the only source is the daily reward in the lobby; they are spent on permanent upgrades (next section).
- **Model** (`Shared.Daily`, all numbers in `Daily.Config`): a personal day counter. Day 1 is the UTC day of the first claim (`Daily.First` = `os.time() // 86400`), day n = today − `First` + 1. One claim per UTC day, by server `os.time()` only. Days 1, 8, 15… (every 7th) give `MilestoneReward` 100, other days `BaseReward` 25. There is no streak reset: a missed day only stays unclaimed. `Daily.Claimed` keeps the claimed personal days of the last 14 days, enough for the window.
- **Claim:** the client calls the RemoteFunction `Lobby.Remotes.ClaimDaily` without arguments (one at a time per player, 1 s cooldown). `DailyServer` writes like the lobby `ShopServer`: `SaveStore.prepare`, `UpdateAsync` with `SaveSchema.upgrade` inside, no yields in the function. A fresh `Session` (a match server holds the data) → `Busy`; today already in `Claimed` → `Already`, so a double click or a second server never pays twice. Answers: `Ok` (with `day` and `reward`), `Already`, `Busy`, `Unavailable`, `NotYet` (only with a negative day shift).
- **Attributes:** `LobbyCashServer` reads `Crystals`, `DailyFirst` (0 = never claimed) and `DailyClaimed` (`"1,2,4"`) on entry; `DailyServer` sets the same after a claim and computes `DailyDay` and `DailyReady` from them (no DataStore read) on entry, after a claim, at every UTC midnight and when `DebugDayOffset` changes.
- **Window** (`ReplicatedStorage.Lobby.DailyGui`, opened by the HUD button and the gazebo prompt): 8 cards 2×4 from the latest milestone to the next one; a check on claimed days, a cross and faded paper on missed days, today green and pulsing, milestones amber with a gift. The countdown to the next UTC midnight uses `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`. With a gamepad the Claim (or Close) button is selected on open, ButtonB closes. The look was checked on a Studio screenshot 2026-11-09; `[unverified]` the gamepad (no controller tried).
- **Gazebo spot:** `Blockout.World.Gazebo.Floor.DailySpot` holds the `DailyPrompt` prompt and the `DailyMarker` billboard (details in `docs/studio-world.md`); the gift icon rocks while a reward is ready. The prompt opens the window (Play 2026-11-09, `InputHoldBegin` from the Client).
- **Test in Studio:** `DebugDayOffset` shifts "today" (CLAUDE.md, "Daily reward test").

## Permanent upgrades (`StatServer`, `Lobby.StatsGui`, 2026-11-09)

- Four stats bought with Crystals, 5 levels each, no random rolls. All numbers are in `Shared.Crystals` (`Crystals.Stats`, `BASE_WALK_SPEED`); names and texts are `Stat.<Id>.Name/.Desc/.Effect` in Locale.

| Stat (`Id`) | Per level | At level 5 | Prices, Crystals | Where it applies (match) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Pockets (`DeepPockets`) | +5 backpack capacity | +25 | 10 / 25 / 45 / 70 / 100 | `Config.getBackpackCapacity(level, passMultiplier, bonus)`: added to the base before the Big Backpack multiplier, in `LeaderstatsServer`, `UpgradeShopServer` and the match `ShopServer` |
| Snow Boots (`SnowBoots`) | +1 WalkSpeed | 16 → 21 | 10 / 25 / 45 / 70 / 100 | `LeaderstatsServer` sets `Humanoid.WalkSpeed` on every spawn |
| Strong Arms (`StrongArms`) | +5% cut speed, all tools | +25% | 10 / 25 / 45 / 70 / 100 | `Config.getToolStats(tool, levels, cutMultiplier)`: `MeltRate`, `ClickRate` and `Speed` (PileTake); `BlowServer` passes it |
| Tip Jar (`TipJar`) | +10% Cash | +50% | 20 / 45 / 75 / 110 / 150 | `Shop.cashMultiplier`, multiplied with x2 Cash, VIP, boost and Blizzard (also shown in the match HUD) |

- **Buying (lobby only):** the client calls the RemoteFunction `Lobby.Remotes.BuyStat(statId, expectedLevel)` with the level it wants to buy. `StatServer` buys only if the stored level is `expectedLevel − 1`, below the maximum, and the Crystals cover the price from the server config. The debit and the new level are one `UpdateAsync` (same pattern as the daily claim: schema upgrade inside, fresh `Session` → `Busy`, per-player busy flag, 0.5 s cooldown). Answers: `Ok`, `AlreadyOwned` (level mismatch, also a repeated click), `NotEnough`, `Max`, `Busy`, `Unavailable`. After the write it sets `Crystals` and `Stat_<Id>`.
- **Attributes:** `Stat_<Id>` (level 0–5). In the lobby `LobbyCashServer` and `StatServer` set them; in the match `PlayerData.load` reads `Stats` (cleaned by `Crystals.cleanStats`: unknown ids dropped, levels clamped) and `LeaderstatsServer` sets them. The match save never writes `Stats` (tested 2026-11-09).
- **Window** (`ReplicatedStorage.Lobby.StatsGui`): opened by the Upgrades button in the left HUD column and the `StatsPrompt` at the Upgrades stall (`docs/studio-world.md`). Four cards: icon, name, description, 5 pips, current → next effect, a Buy button with the gem and the price (gray when short, amber MAX at the top), the balance in the header. With a gamepad the first card's button is selected on open, ButtonB closes. `[unverified]` the gamepad.

## Other

- `SnowfallClient`: one invisible Part above the camera with a ParticleEmitter; the rate adapts to FPS, on touch screens the start is half as high.
- End of the match ("Play again", GD-5): a choice window `CHOICE_TIME` = 20 s (`workspace.ReturnCountdown`), each player presses "Play again" or "Lobby" on the results card themself (remote `MatchAction`: `again` / `cancel` / `lobby`, once per 0.3 s). There is no host and no vote. The window closes early if everyone has chosen. Those who pressed "Play again" (1–4) go together to a new reserved House Map server (`TeleportOptions.ReservedServerId` = a new GUID, TeleportData `{ map = MatchMap }`), the rest to the lobby. The card reads `PlayAgainVotes`/`PlayAgainTotal` on workspace and `EndChoice` on the player. "Leave to lobby" in the journal (under the tabs, second press within 3 s) sends the same `lobby`.
- Teleport failures: `TeleportAsync` up to 3 attempts 2 s apart. `TeleportInitFailed` is retried only for `Flooded` (after 15 s) and `Failure` (after 2 s), no more than 3 times per player, with the same TeleportOptions (the same group server). "Play again" failed → to the lobby; the lobby failed → kick `Kick.TeleportFailed` (the data is already saved). Those who join after the window are sent to the lobby right away.
